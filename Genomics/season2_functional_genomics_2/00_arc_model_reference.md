Learn about:

[**Arc's Virtual Cell Initiative**](https://arcinstitute.org/virtual-cell-initiative) — Our Institute-wide effort taking a full-stack approach to generate training data and build virtual cell models.

[**Evo 2**](https://arcinstitute.org/tools/evo) — A biological foundation and generative AI model that reads and writes DNA, trained on genetic data from across the tree of life.

[**State**](https://arcinstitute.org/tools/state) — Arc's first virtual cell model, trained on large perturbational datasets to predict how genetic, chemical, and environmental changes shift gene expression across cell types.

[**Stack**](https://arcinstitute.org/news/foundation-model-stack) — A single-cell foundation model that uses in-context learning to predict cellular responses to perturbations never directly measured.

[**scBaseCount**](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.02.27.640494v3) — AI agents that find, clean, and uniformly process single-cell data for model training, part of Arc's Virtual Cell Atlas.

[**Proto**](https://arcinstitute.org/news/proto) — A framework that integrates disparate AI tools for protein, RNA, and gene-regulation design into a single pipeline for multi-modal biological design.

[**CodonFM**](https://arcinstitute.org/news/codonfm-foundation-model-for-codons) — A family of open-source AI models developed with NVIDIA that reveal the grammar underlying codon choice.

[**Arc’s AIxBio Fellows Program**](https://arcinstitute.org/programs/aixbio-fellows) — A remote fellowship for undergraduates working at the interface of AI and biology, pairing student teams with Arc mentors on 6–12 month projects.
